# Proposal — Zarea digital platform upgrade

## 1. Company snapshot
- Pakistan's first listed e-commerce platform (PSX: ZAL, listed 28 Feb 2025). The IPO raised Rs1.03bn at Rs16.50, 1.9× oversubscribed.
- FY26 (year to 30 Jun 2026):
  - Revenue **Rs2,774.78m** (+106.6%)
  - Gross profit Rs887.23m
  - Net profit **Rs1,394.43m** (+107.8%)
  - EPS Rs5.31
  - Total assets Rs4,792m
- IPO funds earmarked: agri-biomass, **24% in-house logistics**, **12% technology**. That is a direct budget line for platform work.

## 4. Why each feature earns money
1. **RFQ bidding.** Large industrial orders such as coal, steel and biomass come in as RFQs, and competition between suppliers lifts conversion. Zarea can take a commission on each award.
2. **Credit for all categories.** Construction buyers are the most credit-hungry. Credit raises order frequency and basket size, and the financing spread is high margin.
3. **Escrow.** It removes first-order risk with new suppliers, which is the main blocker for B2B e-commerce in Pakistan.
4. **Price board, alerts and AI.** Searches like "cement rate today" and "sarya rate" are very common in Pakistan. A live, crawlable board brings organic traffic, and alerts bring users back.
5. **BOQ estimator.** It turns a person building a house into a five-product basket in one click.
6. **Supplier dashboard.** Supplier-maintained prices keep data fresh without manual work, and it becomes the foundation for supplier subscriptions and promoted listings.
7. **Logistics product.** It earns a return on the 24% of IPO capital going into the fleet, and freight becomes a revenue line.
